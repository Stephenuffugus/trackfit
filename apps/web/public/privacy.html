Privacy

Trackfit, a Sky Wolf Studio tool. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Everything you put into Trackfit stays on your device. There is no account, no sign in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send your track list, your photos or anything else to us or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

Trackfit keeps a few things in your browser's own storage on this device (localStorage), so your box is still there next time:

  • your track list: piece names, sizes and how many you own;
  • any photos you take of a piece or a gap (they are kept as pictures on the device only; Trackfit does not look inside them or upload them);
  • the gap you last measured, your units (inches or millimetres);
  • your comfort settings (text size and similar), and whether you have seen the welcome cards.

The app's own files are also saved on the device so it opens with no signal.

To delete everything: use Reset in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser or phone settings, or uninstall the app.

Things that leave the device only when you choose

  • Save a backup file or print / PDF: the file is made on your device and saved where you choose.
  • Where to buy links open a shop's own search page (for example eBay, Walthers, Amazon) in your browser, with the piece name as the search. That shop then sees your visit, under its own privacy policy. Trackfit earns nothing from these links.
  • Report an issue opens your own email app with a message to us. We see only what you send.

Children

Trackfit collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
