# Trackfit — first look

Thanks for being our first tester. Trackfit is a tool for the most
common problem in this hobby: **"I have these track pieces. What can I
build, and what's missing?"** It runs in your phone's web browser — no
app store, no install. Just a website built for the workbench.

This card walks you through the things we'd most like you to try. There
are no right answers and nothing you can break. **Your reactions matter
more than your patience** — if anything is confusing, that's our bug,
not yours.

---

## How to open it

On your phone or tablet, open the browser and go to:

> **trackfit.stevieweedseed.com**

(There's no login. No account to make. No email asked for.)

For the most app-like experience, use your browser's "Add to Home
Screen" option after the page loads — that puts a Trackfit icon on your
home screen so you don't have to find the bookmark next time.

## What to try (about 10 minutes)

### 1. Pick a track system

You'll see a row of chips at the top — Lionel FasTrack, Atlas HO Code
83, Kato Unitrack, and a dozen more. Tap whichever matches what's
actually in your box. The inventory list fills with every piece that
system makes.

### 2. Edit your inventory

For each row, change the **Qty** to how many of that piece you actually
own. Set anything you don't own to 0. The numbers save automatically.

If you've got a piece the list doesn't know about, tap **+ Add row** at
the bottom and type it in.

### 3. Try the photo button

Tap the empty photo box on any row, take a photo of one of your pieces,
and watch what happens. You should see "Identifying…" for a moment, and
then either the row should auto-fill, or a sheet appears asking which
of three guesses is right.

> **Note:** The photo identification is currently running on test data,
> not your real photo. We're checking whether the *experience* feels
> right before turning on the real AI — that part costs money and we
> want to know it's worth paying for first. After three photo
> identifications, the app will show a "Trackfit Premium" upgrade
> screen. **Don't worry about it** — it's there so we can test whether
> the upgrade flow makes sense, but no one will be charged anything
> right now. Just close the screen and keep going.

### 4. Try the gap solver — the main attraction

Scroll down to the **Gap** card. Type in a measurement of a real gap on
your layout (or any number — say, 47 inches). Tap **Solve**.

You should see one or more piece combinations that fit, sorted best to
worst. If nothing fits exactly, you'll see a **SUGGESTED** card telling
you the precise length of one piece that *would* close the gap exactly.

This is the part we're proudest of. Tell us what you think.

### 5. "What can I build with my inventory?"

Below the gap card there's a button: **"What can I build with my
inventory? →"**. Tap it. You should see a list of layouts ranked by
how close you are to building each one. Some you can build today; some
need a couple more pieces.

Try the chips at the top to filter by **Style**, **Footprint**, and
**Scale**. The numbers next to each chip update as you filter.

### 6. Settings (optional)

The gear icon in the header opens a small menu. Three things in there
worth trying:
- **Bigger text** — turns up the font size by 25%.
- **Print inventory report** — generates a PDF binder of every piece
  you own, with photos. Useful for insurance or just for the shelf.
  *(This is a paid feature, so tapping it shows the upgrade screen.
  No charge, just curious what you think of it.)*
- **Show intro again** — replays the welcome cards if you want a
  refresher.

## A note on free vs. paid

Most of Trackfit is **free forever** — the gap solver, the track
libraries, the inventory list, the layout suggester, the cut templates,
photos as visual mnemonic. We mean it. Those are the things that
should help you on the basement floor with a pile of track.

A handful of features are planned as **Trackfit Premium** — photo-ID
auto-fill (after the three free trials), the printable insurance-grade
inventory binder, and the full eight-vendor "where to buy" list. Free
users see the top two vendors, which is plenty for most needs.

We haven't decided on a price yet. **One of the most useful things you
can tell us is what *you* would consider fair.** No pressure. There's
nothing to buy today.
