## Trackfit — the field guide for sectional track

**Photograph the gap. Get every combination that fits — or the one piece
you're missing.** Mobile-first, installable, nothing to learn.

**https://trackfit.stevieweedseed.com/**

### The problem it solves

Every sectional-track hobbyist hits the same wall: track on the floor, a
gap to bridge, and no fast way to know which pieces close it. The
desktop tools (AnyRail, SCARM, RailModeller, XTrkCAD) are powerful CAD
programs with, by universal account, a brutal learning curve — and
they're Windows- or Mac-locked. None of them live in your pocket on the
basement floor.

### What Trackfit does

- **Gap solver** — every straight/curve/turnout combination from *your*
  inventory that bridges the gap.
- **Near-miss** — no exact fit? It names the exact piece you'd need.
  This is the differentiator; nothing else does it as a focused tool.
- **Measure with a coin or bill** — photograph the gap with a reference
  object, get the length.
- **"What can I build?"** — scores 20+ layout templates (incl.
  O-gauge-true footprints) against the track you actually own.
- **AnyRail plan import** — drop in a plan, get a buy-list vs. your box.
- **1:1 cut templates** — printable flex-track cut guides.

### Who it's for

Returning and active hobbyists, average age 56–64, N/HO/OO/O, who want
an answer, not a CAD course.

### The model

**Free:** solver, full track library, inventory, layout matcher, gap
measurement, cut templates, JSON backup. Free, and staying free.

**Founder — one time, $19, no subscription:** printable insurance-grade
inventory binder, full dealer list, photo-ID auto-fill as it rolls out.
Founders keep their price if it rises.

### Honest status

Active, pre-1.0. Library geometry is hand-verified; a SKU/source
re-verification pass is in progress before any "most accurate" claim is
made. Built and maintained by one person who reads the forums and
patches reported errors within the week — in a hobby littered with
abandoned tools, that is the pitch.

## B2B: license the Trackfit Track Library (dataset)

Separate from the app. The library is **16 sectional-track systems**
modeled with consistent geometry, each piece carrying a `source_url` and
a `source_verified_at` date and a typed schema (kind, length, radius,
arc, product code).

Most competing libraries are, by their maintainers' own disclaimers,
"partially incomplete" and "not guaranteed." Trackfit's are narrower but
deep, sourced, and dated.

**Plausible licensees / partners:** JMRI (data-interchange ecosystem),
AnyRail, SCARM, RailModeller, or a hobby retailer wanting an embedded
"will this fit?" widget. Format: typed JSON, documented schema, dated
provenance per piece.

a conversation, not to commodify the asset before the app has proven the
