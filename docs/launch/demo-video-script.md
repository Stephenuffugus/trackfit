# Trackfit — demo video script (45–60s)

The single most shareable moment is **quarter-in-the-gap → exact
combination → the missing-piece sentence**. Lead with that. One take,
phone held over a real piece of plywood with real track. No music bed
louder than a voice, no fast cuts, no hype — the audience is 56–64 and
trusts calm. Vertical (9:16) for forum/phone; also export a 16:9.

Total runtime target: **50 seconds.**

---

### Shot 1 — the problem (0:00–0:08)

**Visual:** Top-down on a board. Two pieces of laid track with an obvious
gap between them. A hand sets a pile of loose track next to it.

**VO:** "You've got track down, a gap to close, and a box of pieces.
Which combination actually fits?"

**On-screen text:** *Trackfit*

---

### Shot 2 — measure with a coin (0:08–0:18)

**Visual:** Hand lays a quarter flat in the gap. Phone (the app) framed
over it; tap the shutter. The measured length appears.

**VO:** "Lay a quarter in the gap and take a photo. That's your
measurement — no tape needed."

**On-screen text:** *Measured: 6.1 in*

---

### Shot 3 — the answer (0:18–0:30)

**Visual:** Results list animates in: 2–3 combinations, each a plain row
("2 × 5″ straight + 1 × 1.5″ straight").

**VO:** "Trackfit lists every combination from the track you actually
own. Pick one, lay it down, done."

---

### Shot 4 — the near-miss (the hook) (0:30–0:42)

**Visual:** Remove a piece from inventory so there's no exact fit.
Re-solve. The near-miss line lands, highlighted:
**"You'd need a 2.5″ straight to close this exactly."**

**VO:** "And when nothing in your box fits exactly — it tells you the one
piece you're missing. That's the part no other tool does."

**On-screen text:** *the missing piece, named*

---

### Shot 5 — close + CTA (0:42–0:50)

**Visual:** Quick pan of the "What can I build?" screen scrolling
buildable layouts, then the home screen.

**VO:** "It's free. Runs in your phone's browser. Trackfit — lay it out
before you lay it down."

**On-screen text:**
*Free · no install · trackfit.stevieweedseed.com*

---

### Notes for the shooter

- Use a real, slightly imperfect layout. Pristine renders read as fake to
  this crowd; a scuffed board reads as trustworthy.
- The near-miss sentence is the climax. Hold on it a full beat longer
  than feels necessary. If you cut anything for time, never cut Shot 4.
- Don't say "AI". Say what it does. The audience cares about the answer,
  not the buzzword.
- One honest sentence is allowed at the end of the description, not the
  video: *"Optional one-time $19 Founder unlock for the extras — no
  subscription."*
- Caption the whole thing (burned-in subtitles). A lot of this audience
  watches muted, and several have hearing loss. This matters.
