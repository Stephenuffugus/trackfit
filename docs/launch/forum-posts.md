> I was born — kept hitting the same wall: a pile of FasTrack on the
> basement floor, a gap between two sections, and no quick way to know
> which combination of pieces would close it. He'd shuffle track for an
> hour. So I built him a little tool.
>
> It got bigger than I expected, so I'm putting it out there for the rest
> of you. It's called **Trackfit** and it's free, in your phone's browser
> — nothing to install:
>
> **https://trackfit.stevieweedseed.com/**
>
> What it does:
> - You tell it what's in your box (or photograph a piece).
> - You measure the gap — lay a quarter or a dollar bill in it and snap a
>   photo, or just type the length.

>   nothing fits exactly it tells you the *one piece you'd need* — e.g.
>   "you'd need a 2.5″ straight to close this exactly."
> - There's a "what can I build with what I own?" screen too — it now has
>   O-gauge layouts sized for O footprints, not shrunk-down HO.
>
> It's free to use. If it saves you an afternoon and you want to chip in,

> I'd genuinely like your feedback, and **if you catch a track length

## 3. Model Railroad Hobbyist (MRH) — slightly more technical

**Subject:** Trackfit: a deterministic gap solver + near-miss suggester (free, PWA)

> Sharing a tool I've built and use. The core is a subset-sum / curve
> solver: given your sectional inventory and a target gap (length, and
> optionally lateral offset), it enumerates the combinations that bridge
> it. The part I'm proudest of is the **near-miss**: when there's no
> exact solution it reports the specific missing piece — "a 2.375″
> straight closes this exactly" — instead of just failing.
>
> It's deterministic on purpose. No LLM in the solve path; the answers
> are reproducible and explainable, which matters for this hobby.
>
> Free, runs in the browser, installable as a PWA:
> **https://trackfit.stevieweedseed.com/**
>
> 16 track systems, each piece with a source citation and verification
> date. I treat library accuracy as non-negotiable — if you find a wrong
> dimension, post it and I'll correct it and redeploy that week. There's
> a "what can I build" layout matcher and an AnyRail plan-import →
> buy-list against your inventory.
>
> Free core; optional one-time $19 Founder unlock for the inventory
> binder and extras, no subscription. Feedback and error reports welcome
> — that's why I'm posting.
