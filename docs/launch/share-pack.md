# Trackfit: where to share + what to post

A single action list. Work top to bottom at your own pace.

**Before EACH venue:** open its rules page (or a Facebook group's pinned
rules) and confirm self-promotion is allowed. That one step is what
prevents a ban. The research that informed this list was done without live
web access, so treat venue names and sections as a starting point and
confirm the current rules yourself.

**Universal posture that keeps you safe everywhere:** lead with "free, no
install, no account," say plainly that you made it for a friend and figured
you'd share it, keep the optional $19 out of it (or mention it once, only
where promo is clearly allowed), and reply to every response within a day.
That responsiveness is the whole strategy.

**Link to share everywhere:** https://trackfit.stevieweedseed.com/

---

## Order to work through

1. **OGR (O Gauge Railroading) forum**: https://ogrforum.com/ . The founder
   story lands hardest here. Post **draft #1 in `forum-posts.md`** in the
   layout / track-planning area.
2. **Model Train Forum**: https://www.modeltrainforum.com/ . Post **draft #2**
   in the "Layout & Track Design" section. Check its self-promo rule first;
   it is the most likely of the forums to restrict it.
3. **MRH (Model Railroad Hobbyist) forum**: https://forum.mrhmag.com/ . Post
   **draft #3** in the track-planning / software area.
4. **Trains.com / Model Railroader forum**: https://forum.trains.com/ . Post
   **draft #4** in "Layout Design and Track Planning." Kalmbach is a
   publisher, so keep any mention of the $19 out of it.
5. **Facebook groups**: the best age match for your buyers. Apply/join, read
   each group's pinned rules, then use the message below. (Good targets:
   large O-gauge / Lionel groups for the founder-story version, general HO
   and "layout planning" groups for reach.)
6. **YouTube**: comment honestly on existing "track planning software"
   videos (people searching AnyRail / SCARM / RailModeller). Template below.
7. **NMRA division newsletters / local club newsletters**: a personal email
   to an editor, not a blast. Highest trust, lowest volume, very on-audience.
8. **(Optional) Reddit**: see the note and draft at the bottom. Only if you
   want the feedback / word-of-mouth play.

> which combination of pieces will fit. So I built him a free tool for it,

> headache.
>
> It runs right in your phone's browser, nothing to install and no account:
> https://trackfit.stevieweedseed.com/
>
> You tell it what track you own, measure the gap (or photograph it), and it
> lists every combination that closes it. If nothing fits exactly, it tells
> you the one piece you would need. There is also a "what can I build with
> what I have?" screen.
>
> It is free. I made it as a gift and I am just putting it out there. If
> folks find it useful I will keep improving it, so I would genuinely love
> feedback, and if you ever spot a track measurement that looks wrong, tell
> me and I will fix it.

## (Optional) Reddit

Note: `distribution-30-60-90.md` and `forum-posts.md` say "No Reddit." That
was a *sales-channel* call, and it is correct that your $19 buyer is not on
Reddit. But as a *feedback / word-of-mouth / search-visibility* play, one
honest post is worth it. Treat this section as a deliberate, optional
revision of that rule, your call.

If you do it: use your real account (never a throwaway), post once per
subreddit, lead with the maker story, leave the $19 out, and check each
sub's self-promo rule (or DM a mod) before posting. Best order:
**r/modeltrains** (reach) then **r/Lionel** (closest to your O-gauge buyer).
